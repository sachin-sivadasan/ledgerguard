## Brand Context

- **Product:** LedgerSpear — Revenue Intelligence Platform for Shopify App Developers
- **What it does:** Connects to Shopify Partner API, rebuilds revenue ledgers daily, surfaces MRR, churn risk, and AI-powered insights
- **"Ledger"** = financial accuracy, trust, records, a single source of truth
- **"Spear"** = precision, cutting through data noise, directional focus, sharp insight
- **Audience:** Indie and small-team Shopify app developers
- **Tone:** Precise, trustworthy, modern, confident — not corporate or cold
- **Comparable brand aesthetics:** Stripe (clean), Linear (sharp), Vercel (bold minimal)

---

## Core Visual Concept

The logo should merge two ideas:
1. **A spear / upward thrust** — direction, growth, precision
2. **A chart / data element** — revenue, analytics, financial clarity

The spear is not a literal weapon — it's abstracted into a sharp upward line, an arrow, or a directional mark that also reads as a rising trend or data point.

---

## Variation 1: Icon Only (App Icon / Favicon)

**Concepts (pick one per generation):**
- An abstract spearhead pointing upward — clean geometric shape with a sharp apex, could double as an upward arrow or chart peak
- A rising trend line that terminates in a sharp spear tip — data meets precision
- A stylized "L" or "S" shaped as a spear/arrow — monogram approach
- A shield-like shape (guarding revenue) with a spear cutting through it diagonally

**Requirements:**
- NO text, NO letters, NO wordmark — icon only
- Square format (1024x1024)
- Works at 16px (favicon) through 1024px (splash)
- Clean white or transparent background
- Max 2 colors
- Geometric, not illustrative

---

## Variation 2: Wordmark ("LedgerSpear")

**Direction:**
- Clean sans-serif typeface — medium weight, modern
- "Ledger" in one weight, "Spear" slightly bolder or in accent color to emphasize the sharp half
- No effects, no gradients on text — clean type
- The icon can inform subtle type treatment (e.g., the crossbar of the "t" or tail of the "r" angled like a spear tip)

**Requirements:**
- Horizontal format for website header and navbar
- Legible at small sizes
- Works on both light and dark backgrounds

---

## Variation 3: Icon + Wordmark

**Layout:** Icon left, wordmark right — standard SaaS header logo.

**Requirements:**
- Balanced proportion — icon should not overpower text
- Horizontal, suitable for navbar (height ~40px rendered)
- Clear separation between icon and text

---

## Variation 4: Favicon

**Requirements:**
- Simplest form of the icon — single shape, single color
- Recognizable at 16x16 and 32x32
- No fine details — bold silhouette only

| Background | White | #FFFFFF | Clean, professional |

Keep it to **one or two colors max**. The best SaaS logos are single-color and work in monochrome.

---

## Style Direction

- **Geometric and minimal** — built from simple shapes (triangles, lines, angles)
- **Sharp, not rounded** — conveys the "spear" precision
- **Flat design** — no 3D, no shadows, no gradients
- **High contrast** — works in solid black, solid white, and brand blue
- **Timeless** — avoid trendy effects that age quickly
- **Confident negative space** — don't fill every pixel

---

## What to Avoid

- Literal weapon / battle spear imagery
- Overly complex details or illustrations
- Gradients, glows, shadows, or 3D effects
- More than 2 colors
- Rounded/bubbly shapes — doesn't match "spear" precision
- Generic chart/graph icons that look like every other analytics tool
- Text in icon-only versions
- Clip art or stock icon aesthetic
