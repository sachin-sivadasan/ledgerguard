## Prompt

Design a modern app logo for **LedgerGuard**, a Revenue Intelligence Platform for Shopify App Developers.

**Visual concept:** A watercolor paint stroke on a canvas/board — artistic yet professional. The watercolor effect should suggest fluidity and insight (data flowing into clarity).

**Symbol ideas:**
- A shield or guard silhouette rendered in watercolor strokes (ties to "Guard" in LedgerGuard)
- A ledger/book outline with watercolor wash filling it
- An abstract bar chart or upward trend line painted in watercolor style
- A combination: shield + chart/graph elements in watercolor

**Color palette:**
- Primary: Deep blue (#2563EB) — trust, intelligence, finance
- Accent: Teal/cyan wash — analytics, freshness
- Optional warm accent: Gold or amber stroke — revenue, value
- Background: Clean white or transparent

**Style requirements:**
- Watercolor paint texture — visible brush strokes, soft edges, color bleeds
- Looks like paint on a canvas/board surface (subtle paper/board texture)
- Modern and minimal — not overly detailed
- Works at small sizes (app icon, favicon) and large (splash screen)
- Professional B2B SaaS feel — not childish or overly artistic
- Flat/semi-flat design with watercolor texture overlay

**Formats needed:**
- Square app icon (1024x1024)
- Horizontal logo with text "LedgerGuard" beside the icon
- Icon-only variant (no text)

**What to avoid:**
- Photorealistic painting — keep it logo-clean
- Too many colors — max 3
- Generic shield clipart — the watercolor treatment should make it unique
- Overly complex details that disappear at small sizes
